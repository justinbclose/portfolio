I run digital strategy for three luxury brands in La Jolla, from the website and email to social, SEO and paid placements.

Day to day that means planning campaigns, building eblasts and landing pages, managing vendors, and turning GA4 data into the reporting and audits for our Director of Sales and Marketing and the decks our board sees each quarter. I led our Revinate to Ascent360 CRM switch end to end, and I shoot and edit my own photo, video and drone content.

I’m very AI focused: I bring new AI tools into our workflow and teach the team how to use them to get more done. I’m always learning, and I think like an owner. I built and sold a service business, and I still run a property management company. I grew up between Park City, the Vail Valley and San Diego, and I’m happiest on skis.
