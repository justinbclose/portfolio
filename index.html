✱ About

Ocean days, mountain roots.

I’m a marketer with a B.S. in Marketing and a background in luxury hospitality and entrepreneurship.

As Sales & Marketing Coordinator at La Jolla Beach & Tennis Club, I support three brands: a luxury hotel, a members-only beach and tennis club, and an oceanfront fine-dining restaurant. Each has its own audience and voice.

My work is mostly digital: WordPress sites, email in HubSpot and Revinate, web and CRM campaigns, and Google Business and Google Ads. I’ve also co-run a service business, built brands from the ground up, and I shoot and edit my own photo, video and drone content.

Outside work, travel, skiing and mountain culture shape how I tell stories.
